The Seymour High School Archery Team

The SHS Archery Team is dedicated to promoting archery skills and sportsmanship among students. Soaring for excellence in competition and camaraderie. Team members participate in local and regional competitions, striving to improve their accuracy and technique while representing their school with pride.

Practice sessions are held weekly on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 6:00 AM to 8:00 AM, and team members are encouraged to attend all meetings and competitions.
