The Seymour High School Archery Team

The Seymour High School (SHS) Archery Team helps students develop archery skills, accuracy, and sportsmanship. Members compete in local and regional tournaments while representing their school.

Team practices are held on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 6:00 to 8:00 a.m. Members are encouraged to attend practices, team meetings, and competitions.
